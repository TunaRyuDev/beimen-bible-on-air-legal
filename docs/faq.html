1. 我的靈修心得與打卡紀錄儲存在哪裡？換手機時如何備份移轉？ Where are my reflections and check-ins stored? How do I backup & transfer them?

Bible On Air 採「本機優先」設計：您的靈修心得筆記、禱告感言及每日打卡月曆紀錄，一律先儲存於您個人的手機本機資料庫（SQLite），並永久保留您的完整歷史。訪客模式下僅存於本機，不會上傳；若您以 Google / Apple 正式帳號登入，最近 6 個月內的心得與打卡會同步至您個人專屬的雲端空間（Firebase），以便在多部裝置間延續進度。您的資料僅限本人帳號存取，教會同工無權檢視您的個人屬靈筆記。 Bible On Air is designed local-first: your reflections, prayer notes, and daily check-in records are always saved first in the local database (SQLite) on your device, which keeps your complete history permanently. In guest mode they stay on your device only and are never uploaded; if you sign in with a Google / Apple account, the most recent 6 months of notes and check-ins are synced to your personal cloud space (Firebase) so you can continue across devices. Your data is accessible only by your own account, and church staff cannot access your private reflections.

📦
如何匯出備份筆記？ How to Export & Backup Notes?
方式一：雲端備份與還原（限已登入 Google / Apple 帳號）。開啟 App 進入「個人檔案」，點選「雲端備份與還原」，在舊裝置按「匯出備份至雲端」，再於新裝置以同一帳號登入並按「從雲端還原」，即可轉移完整歷史打卡與心得。備份檔自建立起僅 12 小時內可供還原，逾期將自動刪除，請盡快於新裝置完成還原。
方式二：匯出讀經心得。進入「個人檔案」，點選「匯出讀經心得」，系統會將心得打包為 Markdown 檔案之 ZIP 壓縮檔，您可儲存至手機檔案夾、傳送至電腦或以通訊軟體保存（訪客模式亦可使用）。 Option 1: Cloud Backup & Restore (signed-in Google / Apple accounts only). Open Profile and tap "Cloud Backup & Restore". On your old device, choose "Export backup to cloud"; then sign in with the same account on the new device and choose "Restore from cloud" to transfer your full check-in and note history. The backup is restorable for only 12 hours after creation and is then automatically deleted, so please restore promptly on the new device.
Option 2: Export Devotional Notes. Open Profile and tap "Export Devotional Notes". The App packages your notes as Markdown files in a ZIP archive that you can save to local files, a computer, or messaging apps (also available in guest mode).

2. 如何開啟、關閉或調整每日晨更靈修推播提醒？ How do I manage daily devotional push reminders?

Bible On Air 提供每日晨更靈修提醒功能，陪伴您在晨光中以神的話語開始新的一天： Bible On Air offers morning devotional reminders to encourage starting each day anchored in God's Word:

  • 首次使用授權：首次開啟 App 時系統會詢問是否允許發送通知，點選「允許」即可準時接收每日經文與晨更推播。 Initial Permission: When prompted upon initial launch, grant notification permission to receive morning scriptures and alerts.
  • 修改系統推播授權：您可隨時至手機系統「設定」>「通知」> 選擇「Bible On Air」開啟或關閉推播通知權限。 System Settings: You can manage notifications anytime in device Settings > Notifications > Bible On Air.

3. 離線狀態下可以使用哪些功能？ Which features are available while offline?

離線可用：在無網路連線的環境下（如飛航模式、地下室），您手機本機的靈修心得筆記與讀經打卡月曆可正常瀏覽與書寫，已快取的靈修目錄與教會公告亦可瀏覽。寫下的筆記與打卡會先存於本機，待恢復連線後再自動同步至雲端（限已登入正式帳號）。
需要連網：靈修文章內文需連線載入；登入、雲端同步、雲端備份與還原亦需網路連線。建議在 Wi-Fi 或行動網路穩定的環境下使用以獲得最佳體驗。 Available offline: Without a network connection (e.g., airplane mode), your on-device devotional notes and reading check-in calendar can be viewed and edited, and the cached devotional index and church announcements can be browsed. Notes and check-ins you make are saved locally first and automatically synced to the cloud once you are back online (signed-in accounts only).
Requires internet: Devotional article content is loaded online; sign-in, cloud sync, and cloud backup & restore also require a connection. For the best experience, use the App on a stable Wi-Fi or mobile network.

4. 帳號管理與個人資料自主權（含帳號註銷與資料刪除指引） Account Management & Data Rights (Account & Data Deletion Guide)

為保障您的個人資料自主權，並完全遵循 Apple App Store Review Guidelines 5.1.1(v) 與 Google Play 使用者資料政策（Account Deletion Requirement），Bible On Air 提供完整透明的帳號與資料刪除管道。若您未來不再使用本服務，可隨時要求刪除雲端登入帳號及清除手機本機所有紀錄。 In accordance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines 5.1.1(v) and the Google Play User Data Policy (Account Deletion Requirement), Bible On Air provides clear pathways to delete your account and clear all local and cloud authentication data permanently.

⚠️
重要提醒：刪除操作不可逆 Important: Deletion is Permanent and Irreversible
帳號刪除完成後，您存放在雲端（同步資料、備份檔、自訂頭像）及手機本機的靈修心得、感言、讀經打卡月曆紀錄皆會被永久抹除，無法復原。若您希望保留歷年靈修筆記，請於刪除前先至 App「個人檔案」點選「匯出讀經心得」下載備份。 Once deleted, your devotional reflections, prayer notes, and reading check-in history — in the cloud (synced data, backup file, custom avatar) and on your device — will be wiped out permanently. Please use "Export Devotional Notes" beforehand if you wish to retain your notes.

途徑一：App 內即時自主刪除（推薦，立即生效） Method 1: Instant In-App Self-Service Deletion (Recommended)

  1. 開啟 App：在您的 iOS 或 Android 裝置上啟動 Bible On Air。 Open the App: Launch Bible On Air on your iOS or Android device.
  2. 前往個人檔案：點選畫面右上角的大頭貼或選單，進入「個人檔案 (Profile)」頁面。 Go to Profile: Tap your avatar or settings icon in the top corner to enter the Profile screen.
  3. 找到刪除選項：向下滑動至頁面底部的危險操作區，點選紅色的「刪除帳號並清除所有資料」選項。 Locate Deletion Action: Scroll down to the danger zone card highlighted in red and tap "刪除帳號並清除所有資料" (Delete Account & Clear All Data).
  4. 二次確認：在系統跳出的警告對話框中，點選確認刪除。 Confirm: In the confirmation dialog, confirm your intent to delete.
  5. 即刻抹除：系統將自動連線 Firebase 伺服器，刪除您的雲端帳號檔案、同步之心得與打卡紀錄、雲端備份檔及自訂頭像，並註銷帳號認證憑證，同時清空手機本機 SQLite 資料庫（抹除所有個人筆記與打卡紀錄）與頭像資料，隨後自動登出返回初始畫面。 Immediate Execution: The system immediately deletes your cloud account profile, synced notes and check-ins, cloud backup file, and custom avatar, removes your Firebase Auth account, clears your device's local SQLite database (wiping all notes and check-ins) and avatar data, and logs you out.

途徑二：透過電子郵件專人人工申請（免開 App） Method 2: Email Deletion Request (Without Opening App)

若您已經解除安裝應用程式、更換新手機或無法開啟 App，您可透過官方客服信箱提出人工刪除申請： If you have uninstalled the App, lost your device, or cannot access the App, you may submit a manual deletion request by email:

  • 信件主旨：【Bible On Air 帳號與個人資料刪除申請】 Subject Line: [Bible On Air Account & Data Deletion Request]
  • 註冊信箱 / 身分識別：請提供您在 Google 或 Apple 登入時使用的電子郵件地址（若使用 Apple 隱藏信箱，請提供您的轉發代碼或使用者 UID）。 Registered Email / Identifier: Provide your Google or Apple Sign-In email address (or relay email / UID if available).
  • 申請說明：註明「本人申請永久刪除 Bible On Air 應用程式登入帳號與認證資訊」。 Statement: "I request the permanent deletion of my Bible On Air account and all associated authentication credentials."

處理時程：我們將於收到信件後 7 個工作天內 完成身分核對與雲端 Firebase 帳號及雲端資料註銷抹除，並以電子郵件回覆確認信函。 Processing Timeline: Our support team will verify your request and completely revoke your cloud account within 7 business days, notifying you via email upon completion.
