應用程式資訊與特色 Application Overview

關於 Bible On Air About Bible On Air

Bible On Air 是由 財團法人台灣省新竹縣基督教北門聖教會 推出的屬靈靈修行動應用程式，旨在協助弟兄姊妹在每日生活中穩定親近神、研讀經文、默想靈修並記錄屬靈心得。 Bible On Air is a mobile devotional application presented by Northgate Holiness Christian Church, designed to assist brothers and sisters in daily devotional reading, meditation, and spiritual reflection journaling.

🕊️
純淨無廣告・無營利承諾 Pure, Ad-Free & Non-Profit Commitment
本應用程式完全免費提供，內部無任何第三方廣告聯播網（Ad Networks）、無追蹤行銷代碼，亦無任何應用程式內購（In-App Purchases）機制。所有經費與維運均由教會支持。 This application is completely free of charge. It contains no third-party advertising networks, no marketing tracking SDKs, and no in-app purchases. All maintenance is supported by the church ministry.
