1. 引言與政策承諾 Introduction & Our Commitment

歡迎使用 Bible On Air（以下簡稱「本應用程式」或「本服務」）。本應用程式由 財團法人台灣省新竹縣基督教北門聖教會（以下簡稱「本教會」、「我們」）開發並維護，旨在提供會友及廣大弟兄姊妹日常靈修、聖經閱讀及個人屬靈筆記之用。 Welcome to Bible On Air (hereinafter referred to as "the App" or "the Service"), developed and maintained by Northgate Holiness Christian Church ("the Church", "we", "us", or "our"). The App is dedicated to supporting church members and believers in daily devotion, scripture reading, and personal spiritual journaling.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party for marketing purposes. This App contains zero third-party advertisement networks and does not track users across other apps or websites.

2. 我們收集的資訊項目 Information We Collect

本應用程式採「資料最小化」（Data Minimization）原則，僅收集提供靈修核心功能所需之必要資訊： We adhere strictly to the principle of data minimization, collecting only information necessary to deliver core devotional features:

2.1 帳號與身分識別資料 2.1 Account & Identity Information

  • 第三方登入（Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple）：當您選擇以 Google 或 Apple 帳號登入時，系統會經由 Firebase Authentication 接收您的專屬使用者識別碼（User ID / UID）、電子郵件地址（Email）、顯示暱稱（Display Name）及大頭照網址（Photo URL），並將帳號檔案（UID、Email、暱稱、登入方式、最後登入時間）儲存於 Cloud Firestore。若您使用 Apple「隱藏我的電子郵件」功能，我們僅會收到 Apple 指派的隨機轉發信箱。 Third-Party Sign-In (Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple): When logging in via Google or Apple, Firebase Authentication receives a unique User ID (UID), email address, display name, and avatar URL, and an account profile (UID, email, display name, sign-in provider, last login time) is stored in Cloud Firestore. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email" feature, we only receive Apple's randomized relay address.
  • 個人檔案（自訂暱稱與頭像）：您可自訂顯示暱稱，並可選擇預設頭像、登入服務提供之頭像，或從相機／相簿選取照片作為自訂頭像。暱稱會儲存於您的帳號檔案，自訂頭像（裁切後縮圖）會上傳至 Firebase Storage，用於不同裝置間同步。 Profile (Custom Nickname & Avatar): You may set a custom display name and choose a preset avatar, your sign-in provider's avatar, or a photo from your camera / photo library as a custom avatar. The nickname is stored in your account profile, and the custom avatar (cropped thumbnail) is uploaded to Firebase Storage to sync across devices.
  • 訪客模式（Guest Mode）：若您選擇不登入以訪客身分使用，系統僅會產生 Firebase 隨機匿名識別碼，不收集任何個人真實姓名或電子郵件。訪客的靈修心得與打卡紀錄僅存於本機，不會同步至雲端，亦無法使用雲端備份與還原功能。 Guest / Anonymous Mode: If you use the App as a guest, only a randomized anonymous Firebase identifier is generated; no real name or email is collected. A guest's devotional notes and check-ins stay on the device only, are not synced to the cloud, and cannot use Cloud Backup & Restore.

2.2 靈修心得與閱讀活動（本機儲存；登入帳號可雲端同步） 2.2 Devotional Notes & Reading Activity (Stored Locally; Cloud Sync for Signed-In Accounts)

  • 靈修心得與筆記（Devotional Notes）：您針對每日經文所撰寫的感言、禱告與個人心得筆記（每篇上限 512 字）。筆記一律先儲存於您手機的本機資料庫（Local SQLite）；訪客模式下僅存於本機。若您以 Google / Apple 正式帳號登入，最近 6 個月內的筆記會同步至您專屬的 Cloud Firestore 路徑，以便跨裝置使用（詳見第 5 節）。此資料僅供您本人存取，不會公開或與其他使用者分享，教會同工亦無法查看。 Devotional Reflections & Notes: Personal reflections, prayers, and notes you write for daily readings (up to 512 characters each). Notes are always saved first in your local on-device database (Local SQLite); in guest mode they stay on your device only. If you sign in with a Google / Apple account, notes from the most recent 6 months are synced to your personal Cloud Firestore path for cross-device use (see Section 5). This data is accessible only by you; it is never made public or shared with other users, and church staff cannot view it.
  • 閱讀打卡與歷程（Reading Records & Calendar）：記錄您每日完成靈修經文的閱讀狀態與打卡時間戳記，供您檢視個人靈修月曆與進度。其儲存與同步方式與心得筆記相同（本機為主；登入帳號同步近 6 個月）。 Reading Check-ins & Progress: Timestamps of completed daily readings to provide your monthly reading calendar. They are stored and synced in the same way as notes (local-first; recent 6 months synced for signed-in accounts).
  • 雲端備份檔（選用）：僅在您主動使用「雲端備份與還原」時，才會將完整歷史打卡與心得壓縮上傳至 Firebase Storage，並於 12 小時有效期限後無法還原、由系統自動刪除（詳見第 5 節）。 Cloud Backup File (optional): Only when you actively use "Cloud Backup & Restore" is your full history of check-ins and notes compressed and uploaded to Firebase Storage; it can no longer be restored after the 12-hour validity and is automatically deleted (see Section 5).

2.3 技術與裝置資訊 2.3 Technical & Device Information

  • 推播通知代碼（FCM Token）：若您允許接收推播通知，系統會記錄 Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) 代碼，並與您的帳號檔案一同儲存，僅用於向您的裝置發送每日靈修提醒與教會重要公告。 Push Notification Token (FCM Token): If notification permission is granted, a Firebase Cloud Messaging token is registered and stored with your account profile, solely to deliver daily devotional reminders and church announcements.
  • 崩潰日誌與診斷（Crashlytics & Diagnostics）：為維持系統穩定性，當應用程式發生閃退或嚴重異常時，Firebase Crashlytics 會收集錯誤回溯（Stack Trace）、裝置型號及作業系統版本。您亦可在 App 內主動匯出診斷日誌（ZIP 檔）並自行以電子郵件寄送給我們進行問題回報。 Crash Reports & Diagnostics (Crashlytics): To ensure app stability, Firebase Crashlytics collects stack traces, device model, and OS version when crashes occur. You can also manually export a diagnostics log (ZIP) and send it to us by email to report issues.
  • 使用統計（Firebase Analytics）：收集功能使用頻率（如打卡次數、熱門功能開啟），並僅以 Firebase 使用者識別碼（UID）及登入方式關聯，不會傳送您的電子郵件，供工程團隊改善操作體驗。 Usage Analytics (Firebase Analytics): Collects feature usage statistics (e.g., check-in counts, popular features) associated only with your Firebase user ID (UID) and sign-in method — your email is not sent — to help optimize the user experience.

5. 資料安全與保存架構 Data Security & Storage Architecture

雲端與本機分工保護：本應用程式以「本機優先」（Local-First）設計，手機本機資料庫為您的主要資料來源；僅在您以 Google / Apple 正式帳號登入時，才會啟用下列雲端功能。 Hybrid Architecture & Protection: The App is designed local-first: the on-device database is your primary source of data. The cloud features below are enabled only when you sign in with a Google / Apple account.

  • 手機裝置本機：您的靈修筆記、禱告感言與每日打卡紀錄永久保存於本機資料庫（Local SQLite），享有手機作業系統之沙盒隱私保護；訪客模式下，這些資料僅存在您的手機中，不會上傳至任何伺服器。 Local Device: Your devotional notes, prayers, and daily check-ins are kept permanently in the local on-device database (Local SQLite), protected by the operating system sandbox. In guest mode, this data stays on your device and is never uploaded to any server.
  • 雲端跨裝置同步（Cloud Firestore，僅限正式登入帳號）：為方便您在多部裝置間延續靈修進度，已登入之正式帳號會將最近 6 個日曆月內的靈修心得（每篇上限 512 字）與讀經打卡紀錄同步至 Cloud Firestore（儲存於您個人帳號專屬路徑下）。超過同步期間的雲端資料會由系統依到期時間自動清除，但您手機本機的歷史資料不會因此被刪除。刪除的筆記會以「刪除標記」同步至其他裝置，並隨到期自動回收。 Cloud Cross-Device Sync (Cloud Firestore, signed-in accounts only): To let you continue your devotions across devices, signed-in accounts sync devotional notes (up to 512 characters each) and reading check-ins from the most recent 6 calendar months to Cloud Firestore, stored under your personal account path. Cloud data outside this window is automatically purged upon expiry, but your local on-device history is never deleted as a result. Deleted notes are propagated to your other devices as deletion markers, which are also automatically purged upon expiry.
  • 雲端備份與還原（Firebase Storage，僅限正式登入帳號）：當您主動使用「雲端備份與還原」功能時，系統會將您完整歷史之打卡與靈修心得打包為單一壓縮檔，暫存於您專屬之路徑（每位使用者最多 1 份，重複匯出將覆寫）。該備份檔自建立起僅 12 小時內可供還原，逾期即無法還原，並由系統於 1～2 日內自動刪除。 Cloud Backup & Restore (Firebase Storage, signed-in accounts only): When you actively use "Cloud Backup & Restore", your full history of check-ins and devotional notes is packaged into a single compressed file and temporarily stored under your personal path (at most one file per user; re-exporting overwrites it). The backup can be restored for only 12 hours after creation, after which it can no longer be restored and is automatically deleted by the system within 1–2 days.
  • 自訂頭像（Firebase Storage）：若您設定自訂頭像，裁切後之縮圖將上傳至您專屬之 Firebase Storage 路徑，供您在不同裝置間同步顯示；您可隨時改回預設頭像，刪除帳號時亦一併刪除。 Custom Avatar (Firebase Storage): If you set a custom avatar, the cropped thumbnail is uploaded to your personal Firebase Storage path so it can be displayed across your devices. You can switch back to a preset avatar at any time, and it is deleted along with your account.
  • 存取權限與加密：您的雲端同步資料、備份檔與頭像，僅限您本人之登入帳號可讀寫，本教會同工及管理後台人員均無權限讀取您的靈修心得、打卡紀錄與備份檔；所有資料傳輸全程採用 HTTPS / TLS 加密。 Access Control & Encryption: Your cloud-synced data, backup file, and avatar can be read and written only by your own signed-in account. Church staff and administrators have no access to your devotional notes, check-ins, or backup file. All data in transit is protected with HTTPS / TLS encryption.

保存期限：帳號檔案與認證資料於帳號存續期間保存；同步之心得與打卡資料依上述 6 個月視窗滾動保存；雲端備份檔 12 小時內可還原並於 1～2 日內自動刪除；自訂頭像保存至您更換或刪除帳號為止。一旦您執行帳號刪除，上述雲端資料與 Firebase Authentication 帳號將一併刪除，手機本機資料庫亦同步清空。 Retention Period: Account profile and authentication data are kept while your account remains active; synced notes and check-ins are retained on a rolling 6-month window; cloud backups are restorable for 12 hours and automatically deleted within 1–2 days; custom avatars are kept until you change them or delete your account. Upon account deletion, all of the above cloud data and your Firebase Authentication account are deleted, and the local database is cleared.

6. 使用者權利與資料刪除機制 Your Rights & Data Deletion

依據個資法規，您對您的個人資料享有以下完整權利： Under data protection laws, you possess the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • 查詢與閱覽：您可隨時在 App 內檢視個人帳號身分、暱稱頭像、靈修心得與打卡紀錄。 Access & Review: You can view your account identity, nickname, avatar, devotional notes, and reading check-ins inside the App at any time.
  • 匯出與備份資料：App 內提供「匯出讀經心得」功能（將心得打包為 Markdown 檔案之 ZIP 壓縮檔，供您保存或分享），亦提供「雲端備份與還原」功能（限已登入正式帳號）供跨裝置轉移完整歷史紀錄。 Data Export & Backup: The App provides an "Export Devotional Notes" feature (a ZIP archive of Markdown files for you to save or share) and, for signed-in accounts, a "Cloud Backup & Restore" feature to transfer your full history across devices.
  • 自主刪除帳號與資料（自服務）：您可直接在 App 內點選「個人檔案 (Profile)」→「刪除帳號並清除所有資料」，經二次確認後，系統將刪除您在雲端的全部資料——包括 Firestore 中之帳號檔案、同步之靈修心得與打卡紀錄、Firebase Storage 中之雲端備份檔與自訂頭像——並註銷 Firebase Authentication 登入帳號，同時清空手機本機資料庫中的所有心得、打卡紀錄與頭像資料。 Self-Service Account & Data Deletion: Navigate to Profile → "Delete Account & Clear All Data" and confirm. The App deletes all of your cloud data — including your account profile, synced devotional notes and reading check-ins in Firestore, your cloud backup file and custom avatar in Firebase Storage — and removes your Firebase Authentication account, while also clearing all notes, check-ins, and avatar data from the local device database.
  • 專人協助刪除：若您無法開啟 App 或已將其解除安裝，可隨時寄信至 support@tuna-ryu.cc，我們將於 7 個工作天內由專人為您完成雲端登入帳號及雲端資料之註銷抹除。詳細流程請參閱常見問題之帳號與資料刪除指引。 Manual Deletion Assistance: If you cannot access the App or have uninstalled it, email support@tuna-ryu.cc. We will revoke your cloud account and purge your cloud data within 7 business days. See our FAQ: Account & Data Deletion Guide.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or applicable age in your jurisdiction) without verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such information.
