Bible On Air 帳號與個人資料刪除指引 Account & Data Deletion Instructions

📅 最後更新與生效日期：2026 年 10 月Effective Date: October 2026 🏛️ 主辦單位：財團法人台灣聖教會 新竹北門聖教會Organization: Taiwan Holiness Church Hsinchu Beimen Holiness Church

1. 資料刪除承諾 Our Commitment to Data Rights

為保障您的個人資料自主權，並完全遵循 Apple App Store Review Guidelines 5.1.1(v) 與 Google Play 使用者資料政策（User Data Policy - Account Deletion Requirement），Bible On Air 提供完整透明的帳號與資料刪除管道。無論您身在何處，皆可隨時要求刪除雲端登入帳號及清除手機本機所有紀錄。 In accordance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines 5.1.1(v) and the Google Play User Data Policy (Account Deletion Requirement), Bible On Air provides clear, straightforward pathways to delete your account and clear all local and cloud authentication data permanently.

⚠️
重要提醒：刪除操作不可逆 Important: Deletion is Permanent and Irreversible
帳號刪除完成後，您存放在手機本機的靈修心得、感言、讀經打卡月曆紀錄皆會被永久抹除，無法復原。若您希望保留歷年靈修筆記，建議您在執行刪除前，先至 App「個人檔案」點選「匯出全部心得為 ZIP 檔」下載備份。 Once deleted, your devotional reflections, prayer notes, and reading check-in calendar history on your device will be wiped out permanently. If you wish to keep your notes, please use the "Export All Notes to ZIP" feature in your Profile before proceeding with deletion.

2. 途徑一：App 內即時自主刪除（推薦，立即生效） Method 1: Instant In-App Self-Service Deletion (Recommended)

您可以在手機上的 Bible On Air 應用程式內直接完成即時刪除，無需等待人工審核： You can delete your account and data directly within the Bible On Air mobile app instantly without waiting:

  1. 開啟 App：在您的 iOS 或 Android 裝置上啟動 Bible On Air。 Open the App: Launch Bible On Air on your iOS or Android device.
  2. 前往個人檔案：點選畫面右上角的大頭貼或選單，進入「個人檔案 (Profile)」頁面。 Go to Profile: Tap your avatar or settings icon in the top corner to enter the Profile screen.
  3. 找到刪除選項：向下滑動至頁面底部的危險操作區，點選紅色的「刪除帳號並清除所有資料」選項。 Locate Deletion Action: Scroll down to the danger zone card highlighted in red and tap "刪除帳號並清除所有資料" (Delete Account & Clear All Data).
  4. 二次確認：在系統跳出的警告對話框中，點選確認刪除。 Confirm: In the confirmation dialog, confirm your intent to delete.
  5. 即刻抹除：系統將自動連線 Firebase 伺服器註銷並刪除您的帳號認證憑證，同時清空手機本機 SQLite 資料庫（抹除所有個人筆記與打卡紀錄），隨後自動登出返回初始畫面。 Immediate Execution: The system immediately deletes your Firebase Auth account, clears your device's local SQLite database (wiping all notes and check-ins), and logs you out to the splash screen.

3. 途徑二：透過電子郵件專人人工申請（免開 App） Method 2: Email Deletion Request (Without Opening App)

若您已經解除安裝應用程式、更換新手機或無法開啟 App，您可透過官方客服信箱提出人工刪除申請： If you have uninstalled the App, lost your device, or cannot access the App, you may submit a manual deletion request by email:

📧
專人刪除申請信箱： Dedicated Deletion Support Email:
support@tuna-ryu.cc

信件填寫內容規範： Required Information in Email:

  • 信件主旨：【Bible On Air 帳號與個人資料刪除申請】 Subject Line: [Bible On Air Account & Data Deletion Request]
  • 註冊信箱 / 身分識別：請提供您在 Google 或 Apple 登入時使用的電子郵件地址（若使用 Apple 隱藏信箱，請提供您的轉發代碼或使用者 UID）。 Registered Email / Identifier: Provide your Google or Apple Sign-In email address (or relay email / UID if available).
  • 申請說明：註明「本人申請永久刪除 Bible On Air 應用程式登入帳號與認證資訊」。 Statement: "I request the permanent deletion of my Bible On Air account and all associated authentication credentials."

處理時程：我們將於收到信件後 7 個工作天內 完成身分核對與雲端 Firebase 帳號註銷抹除，並以電子郵件回覆確認信函。 Processing Timeline: Our support team will verify your request and completely revoke your cloud account within 7 business days, notifying you via email upon completion.
