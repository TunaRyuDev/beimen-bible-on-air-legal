Bible On Air 隱私權政策 Bible On Air Privacy Policy

1. 引言與政策承諾 Introduction & Our Commitment

歡迎使用 Bible On Air（以下簡稱「本應用程式」或「本服務」）。本應用程式由 財團法人台灣聖教會 新竹北門聖教會（以下簡稱「本教會」、「我們」）開發並維護，旨在提供會友及廣大弟兄姊妹日常靈修、聖經閱讀、信息聆聽及個人屬靈筆記之用。 Welcome to Bible On Air (hereinafter referred to as "the App" or "the Service"), developed and maintained by Taiwan Holiness Church Hsinchu Beimen Holiness Church ("the Church", "we", "us", or "our"). The App is dedicated to supporting church members and believers in daily devotion, scripture reading, audio sermons, and personal spiritual journaling.

我們深知隱私與個人資料的重要性，並致力依據中華民國《個人資料保護法》、Apple App Store 審查指南第 5.1 條及 Google Play 使用者資料政策，透明公開我們處理資料的方式。我們絕不會販售、出租或出於商業行銷目的向任何第三方揭露您的個人資料。本服務無任何廣告聯播網，亦不參與任何跨應用程式之廣告追蹤。 We deeply value your privacy and are committed to complying with Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act, Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1, and Google Play User Data Policies. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party for marketing purposes. This App contains zero third-party advertisement networks and does not track users across other apps or websites.

2. 我們收集的資訊項目 Information We Collect

本應用程式採「資料最小化」（Data Minimization）原則，僅收集提供靈修核心功能所需之必要資訊： We adhere strictly to the principle of data minimization, collecting only information necessary to deliver core devotional features:

2.1 帳號與身分識別資料 2.1 Account & Identity Information

  • 第三方登入（Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple）：當您選擇以 Google 或 Apple 帳號登入時，系統會經由 Firebase Authentication 接收您的專屬使用者識別碼（User ID / UID）、電子郵件地址（Email）、顯示暱稱（Display Name）及大頭照網址（Photo URL）。若您使用 Apple「隱藏我的電子郵件」功能，我們僅會收到 Apple 指派的隨機轉發信箱。 Third-Party Sign-In (Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple): When logging in via Google or Apple, Firebase Authentication receives a unique User ID (UID), email address, display name, and avatar URL. If you utilize Apple's "Hide My Email" feature, we only receive Apple's randomized relay address.
  • 訪客模式（Guest Mode）：若您選擇不登入以訪客身分使用，系統僅會在裝置本地與 Firebase 產生隨機匿名識別碼，不收集任何個人真實姓名或電子郵件。 Guest / Anonymous Mode: If you use the App as a guest, a randomized anonymous identifier is generated. No real name or email is collected.

2.2 靈修心得與閱讀活動（僅儲存於本機裝置） 2.2 Devotional Notes & Reading Activity (Stored Locally on Device Only)

  • 靈修心得與筆記（Devotional Notes）：您針對每日經文所撰寫的感言、禱告與個人心得筆記。本應用程式極度重視您的隱私，所有靈修心得筆記皆僅儲存在您自己的手機裝置本機資料庫中（Local SQLite），雲端伺服器不會收集、同步或儲存您的筆記內容。 Devotional Reflections & Notes: Personal reflections, prayers, and notes you write for daily readings. To protect your privacy, all devotional notes are stored exclusively in your local on-device database (Local SQLite). The cloud server does not collect, sync, or store your notes.
  • 閱讀打卡與歷程（Reading Records & Calendar）：記錄您每日完成靈修經文的閱讀狀態與打卡時間戳記，供您在手機上檢視個人靈修月曆與進度。此打卡歷程同樣僅儲存於您的手機本機，雲端伺服器不保存您的個人閱讀歷程。 Reading Check-ins & Progress: Timestamps of completed daily readings to provide monthly reading calendars on your device. These check-in records are likewise stored solely on your local device and are not uploaded to cloud servers.

2.3 技術與裝置資訊 2.3 Technical & Device Information

  • 推播通知代碼（FCM Token）：若您允許接收推播通知，系統會記錄 Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) 代碼，僅用於向您的裝置發送每日靈修提醒與教會重要公告。 Push Notification Token (FCM Token): If notification permission is granted, a Firebase Cloud Messaging token is registered solely to deliver daily devotional reminders and church announcements.
  • 崩潰日誌與診斷（Crashlytics & Diagnostics）：為維持系統穩定性，當應用程式發生閃退或嚴重異常時，Firebase Crashlytics 會收集匿名錯誤回溯（Stack Trace）、裝置型號及作業系統版本。您亦可在 App 內主動匯出診斷日誌進行問題回報。 Crash Reports & Diagnostics (Crashlytics): To ensure app stability, Firebase Crashlytics collects anonymized stack traces, device model, and OS version when crashes occur. You can also manually export diagnostics to report issues.
  • 基本彙整使用統計（Firebase Analytics）：收集去識別化之功能使用頻率（如打卡次數、熱門功能開啟），供工程團隊改善操作體驗。 Aggregated Analytics (Firebase Analytics): Collects de-identified usage statistics (e.g., feature frequency) to optimize user experience.
ℹ️
我們「絕不」收集的機敏資訊 Data We Do NOT Collect
本 App 絕不收集：精確或粗略地理位置 (GPS)、通訊錄聯絡人、相機拍照或相簿檔案、麥克風錄音、信用卡或任何財務付款資料、以及廣告追蹤識別碼 (IDFA / AAID)。 We NEVER collect: Precise/coarse GPS location, Contacts/Address book, Camera or Photo library, Microphone audio, Credit cards or payment information, or Advertising Tracking Identifiers (IDFA / AAID).

3. 個人資料之使用目的 How We Use Your Information

我們收集上述資料之法定目的僅限於： We use collected information solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  1. 提供、維持並個人化 Bible On Air 應用程式服務（包括靈修內容呈現、經文音訊播放）。 Provide, maintain, and personalize the Bible On Air application (including scripture display and audio playback).
  2. 進行使用者身分認證與帳號安全管理（經由 Firebase Authentication）。 Provide user authentication and secure account access management (via Firebase Authentication).
  3. 依據您的偏好設定發送晨更提醒與教會重要活動通知。 Deliver devotional reminders and church announcements according to your notification preferences.
  4. 分析與排除軟體錯誤，提升應用程式穩定性與資安防護（Firebase App Check）。 Analyze errors, diagnose technical glitches, and enhance security and abuse prevention (Firebase App Check).
  5. 回覆您的客服詢問與問題診斷協助。 Respond to your customer support inquiries and provide diagnostic troubleshooting.

5. 資料安全與保存架構 Data Security & Storage Architecture

雲端與本機分工保護： Hybrid Architecture & Protection:

  • 雲端伺服器（僅限登入認證）：雲端僅使用 Google Firebase Authentication 儲存您用於登入的基本帳號識別資訊（如 UID、Email），傳輸過程全程強制採用 HTTPS / TLS 1.3 企業級加密協定。雲端不儲存您的任何閱讀打卡紀錄或靈修心得筆記。 Cloud Server (Authentication Only): The cloud utilizes Google Firebase Authentication solely to store essential login identifiers (UID, Email), enforced with HTTPS/TLS 1.3 enterprise encryption. Cloud servers do not store your reading records or devotional notes.
  • 手機裝置本機（心得筆記與打卡）：您的靈修筆記、感言禱告與每日打卡紀錄完全儲存在您個人手機之本機資料庫中（Local SQLite），享有手機作業系統最高級別之沙盒隱私保護，不會上傳至任何伺服器。 Local Device (Notes & Check-ins): Your devotional reflections, prayer journals, and daily reading check-ins reside strictly within your local on-device database (Local SQLite), protected by the operating system sandbox and never transmitted to remote servers.

保存期限：雲端帳號憑證於帳號存續期間維持；一旦您執行帳號刪除，Firebase Auth 雲端帳號即刻永久註銷，手機本機資料庫亦一併清空抹除。 Retention Period: Cloud authentication credentials persist while your account remains active. Upon initiating account deletion, cloud credentials are permanently revoked immediately, and the local SQLite database is cleared.

6. 使用者權利與資料刪除機制 Your Rights & Data Deletion

依據個資法規，您對您的個人資料享有以下完整權利： Under data protection laws, you possess the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • 查詢與閱覽：您可隨時在 App 內檢視個人帳號身分與本機靈修心得。 Access & Review: You can view your account identity and local devotional notes inside the App at any time.
  • 匯出本機資料：App 內提供「匯出全部心得筆記為 ZIP 檔」功能，方便您隨時將手機內的心得備份至個人電腦或雲端硬碟。 Data Portability: The App provides a built-in feature to export all devotional notes into a ZIP archive for offline backup.
  • 自主刪除帳號與資料（自服務）：您可直接在 App 內點選「個人檔案 (Profile)」→「刪除帳號並清除所有資料」，系統將立即自 Firebase Auth 徹底註銷並抹除您的登入帳號，同時徹底清空手機本機 SQLite 資料庫中的所有心得與打卡紀錄。 Self-Service Account & Data Deletion: Navigate to Profile → "Delete Account & Clear All Data". The App instantly permanently deletes your Firebase Auth account and clears all local notes and check-ins from the device SQLite database.
  • 專人協助刪除：若您無法開啟 App 或已將其解除安裝，可隨時寄信至 support@tuna-ryu.cc，我們將於 7 個工作天內由專人為您完成雲端登入帳號註銷抹除。詳細流程請參閱帳號刪除指引。 Manual Deletion Assistance: If you cannot access the App or have uninstalled it, email support@tuna-ryu.cc. We will revoke and purge your cloud account within 7 business days. See our Account Deletion Guide.

7. 未成年人隱私保護 Children's Privacy Protection

本應用程式內容皆為聖經經文、靈修閱讀與教會資訊，屬普遍級內容，適合全家庭與各年齡層會友使用。我們不會在未獲得父母或法定監護人同意之情況下，主動收集 13 歲（或您所在司法管轄區規定之年齡）以下兒童之個人身分資訊。若您發現未成年子女在未經同意下提供了個人資訊，請與我們聯繫，我們將迅速移除相關資料。 Bible On Air content consists of scripture, devotions, and church updates suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or applicable age in your jurisdiction) without verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such information.
